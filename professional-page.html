About the Institute

Founded in 1984, Northbridge Institute of Technology offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across engineering, computing and applied sciences. Every student completes a supervised project with an industry or research partner before graduating.

The campus has twelve laboratories, a central library open until midnight and on-site accommodation for 1,800 students.

Programmes

Four-year undergraduate degrees and two-year master's programmes.

B.Tech Computer Science

Algorithms, systems, data and software engineering.

B.Tech Electronics

Embedded systems, signal processing and VLSI.

B.Tech Mechanical

Design, thermal systems and manufacturing.

M.Sc Data Science

Statistics, machine learning and applied analytics.
